Need a hand?

How to play

Allow camera access, face the front camera, and follow the short movement tutorial. Tilt left or right, duck, or lift your head to dodge. Hold the pose until the punch passes.

Camera or tracking issues

Use a compatible iPhone with AR face tracking support. Make sure the front camera can see your face clearly. If you denied access, enable Camera for Slip! in iPhone Settings and reopen the app.

Contact
